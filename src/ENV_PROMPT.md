Watch samples several frames within each delivery interval (default 4 frames within 15 seconds), then sends one mosaic. Its first receipt says collecting and has no image; the initial frame remains local until the mosaic is ready. Repeated watch calls restart collection. Playback ending early flushes the frames already collected and stops watching. Later scenes share the preceding scene's final boundary frame. Completed scenes are queued in order. Playback pauses during mosaic/subtitle/danmaku processing and when four scenes await delivery, then resumes automatically, so a slow command or consumer does not silently skip that playback time. The wall-clock delivery interval can therefore be longer than configured. Read frameCount and frameTimes: video-frame means one frame, video-mosaic means more than one. Frames follow left-to-right, top-to-bottom order. Never describe several moments when only one frame was delivered. Stalled playback, looping or early completion can yield fewer distinct frames; discrete sampling can miss short actions.
